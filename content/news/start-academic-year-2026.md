## The topo library 📚

Did you know that LUAK members can see all the LUAK topos on the website! You can find the digital topo library under the member section. There you can:

- See what topo's LUAK has in its library.
- Search topos by title, country, type of climbing, etc.

If you have any suggestions or if you want to help with the development of the website, you can contact Hektor on whatsapp.

## Activities

The new academic year is closing in and with that also the start of a new luak year. This means of course new
initiations, info evenings, lots of climbing and lots of fun. Starting from 24 September our regular club evenings will
once again be on Thursday evening from 20h30 to at least 23h00.

### Info evening

For people that are new to LUAK, make sure to come to one of our info evenings on:

- Thursday 24 September
- Monday 28 September

They will start at **20:00** and will be held at the [LUAK clubhouse](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ccjoQiijrza8ceUb7).

### The initiations will be on

- Saturday 3 Ocotber
- Sunnday 11 October

**Signing up is mandatory** and can be done during our info evening and will also be posted on the website after the
info evening (if there is still place).

### More climbing activities

- LUAK 55 year weekend🎉 @Freyr: 26-27 September
- Pont-a-Lesse weekend: 24-25 October
