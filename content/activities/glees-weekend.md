On the long weekend of 31 October -  2 November we are planning to go bouldering in Glees, a bouldering area in Germany on basalt rocks.
Since we go there for bouldering, you don't need any climbing experience to join. Just make sure you have some climbing shoes and outdoor clothes. If you have any crashpads make sure to take them, since we don't have that many at LUAK.

We sleep on a camping, so make sure you have a **warm enough** sleeping bag, matress and a stove. Luak will make the reservation for the group. We can discuss the distribution for tents via whatsapp (you'll get an invitation when you register). For the transport we have the KUL vans and carpooling.

More info & registration in [this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd4GdQI40-j5BzNUP9YxZftYASBWtnO-jvUimyer13gBDWnng/viewform?usp=publish-editor)
