The KVB1 exam is an evaluation of safe toproping skills. More info and overview of the skills you need to demonstrate at [Klimvaardigheidsbewijs (KVB) | Klim- en bergsportfederatie](https://www.klimenbergsport.be/kvb). Note that for the LUAK exam, you will need to belay with all three types of belay device (tuber, autotuber AND semi-automatic). There will be some time before the exam (starting at 16h) where you'll be able to ask questions.

Price? - LUAK membership is compulsory to participate

- student, KBF member: €21
- student, non-KBF member: €25
- non-student (incl. PhD candidates), KBF member: €25
- non-student (incl. PhD candidates), non-KBF member: €29
