The weekend of 24-25 October we are heading to Pont-a-Lesse. This weekend is meant for beginners and beginner lead climbers. We will not give a full KVB2 course, but we'll happily explain everything you need to know in order to lead climb safely on the rocks (so at least knowing how lead climbing works is a plus). Of course experienced climbers can also join!
